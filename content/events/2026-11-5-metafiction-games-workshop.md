Come join for a workshop which will analyze games as unique pieces of art, teaching some methods to analyze and create them. Additionally, we will explore what metafiction is, and how games are uniquely positioned to implement it, with case studies such as Undertale, FF7 Remake, Expedition 33 and The Stanley Parable.
