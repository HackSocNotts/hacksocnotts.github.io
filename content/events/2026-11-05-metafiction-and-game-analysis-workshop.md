This workshop will discuss games as unique pieces of art, and the possibilities this medium brings, looking at architectures, systems and mechanics from a different perspective. As well, this workshop will introduce metafiction and the unique way games are equipped to implement it, with case studies such as Undertale, FF7 Remake, Expedition 33 and The Stanley Parable.
