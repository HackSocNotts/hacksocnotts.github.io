Academic Talk

A talk by Dr Xavier Carpent from the University of Nottingham’s School of Computer Science. In Dr Xavier’s words:

“In this talk, we will introduce pre-image search, a fundamental problem in cryptography, and explore some of its implications. We will look at iterated random functions and use them to construct time-memory trade-offs to answer pre-image queries.

Through relatively simple maths and some hands-on Python experiments, we will rediscover some of the techniques behind attacks such as the A5/1 GSM attack, NTLM password cracking, and a 2024 passive attack on 2.5G networks.”

Hope to see as many of you there for this exciting talk!
